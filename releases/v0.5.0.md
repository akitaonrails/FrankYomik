# v0.5.0

## Read the original, peek the translation

Both clients now leave the page in its original language and reveal the
translation through a magnifier lens. **Press and hold for 200ms** anywhere on
a translated page: a circular lens opens under your finger, follows it while
you hold, and closes when you let go. It is meant for reading in Japanese or
Korean and looking up only the balloon you cannot parse.

- Magnification cycles through 1.5x / 2x / 3x, and the setting sticks.
- A quick tap is untouched, so Kindle still turns pages and webtoons still
  scroll; letting go of a peek does not turn the page either.
- Full-page replacement is still available as a mode — the app's in-page
  toolbar, the extension's popup.

## Memory

Translated pages are whole PNGs, and nothing was discarding them. A long
session grew by a few MB per page and never shrank.

- The app keeps page bytes for the 20 most recent renders and releases each
  captured source once its render reaches the local cache.
- Lens registrations are capped at 8 pages in both clients, and Kindle
  releases every other page on each turn.
- The extension revokes full-page renders per page instead of holding every
  one until the tab closes, and keeps whole-page debug images for the 3 most
  recent pages rather than 40.

## Prefetch

Webtoon submissions now run further ahead of the reader: the app queues 8 pages
at a time and refills within 4 of the frontier, and the extension prefetches
images within 2400px of the viewport. Kindle cannot be prefetched — only the
page you have open exists in the reader's DOM — but lens mode means the
original stays readable while its translation is still queued.

## Under the hood

- Flutter and Go dependencies updated within their major versions.
- `docker-compose.prod.yml` now matches the hardened live deployment.
- Windows builds compile again against the current MSVC toolchain.
